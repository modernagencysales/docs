---
title: User management
description: Invite teammates, manage roles, and track pending invitations.
---

Workspace admins manage team membership from **Settings**. The Invitations card creates pending invites and generates a copyable accept link for each one, and the Members list shows everyone who has joined the workspace along with their name and email.

<Info>
  Only workspace **admins** can view or use the Invitations card. Members with the editor, reviewer, or viewer role won't see it.
</Info>

## Roles

When you invite someone, you pick the role they'll be granted once they accept:

| Role | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Manage members, invitations, and workspace settings. |
| **Editor** | Create and edit content across the workspace. |
| **Reviewer** | Review and comment; limited editing. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access. |

The **Owner** role isn't assignable through invitations — every workspace has exactly one owner, and ownership moves via the separate transfer-ownership flow.

## Inviting a teammate

1. Go to **Settings** and find the **Invitations** card.
2. Enter the invitee's email address.
3. Choose a role from the dropdown (admin, editor, reviewer, or viewer).
4. Click **Send invitation**.

The new invitation appears in the pending list below the form with the assigned role and an expiry label (for example, *Expires in 7 days*).

<Info>
  Maestro does not send invitation emails. You share the accept link with the invitee yourself — over email, chat, or any other channel.
</Info>

## Sharing the accept link

Each pending invitation has a **Copy invite link** button. The link looks like:

```text
https://your-workspace.example.com/invitations/<invitationId>
```

Send this URL to the invitee. When they open it, they'll be prompted to sign in (or create an account) with the email address the invitation was sent to. The backend checks that the signed-in user's verified email matches the invitation before it will accept — if someone else opens the link, they get a generic "not accessible" error and no membership is created.

## Accepting or declining

On the invitation page (`/invitations/<invitationId>`), the invitee sees:

- **Accept invitation** — creates their membership at the invited role and drops them into the workspace.
- **Decline** — closes out the invitation without joining.

Once accepted, the invitee is redirected into the workspace and appears in the Members card immediately. A declined invitation is finalized and can't be reused; issue a new one if the person changes their mind.

## Managing pending invitations

From the Invitations card an admin can:

- **Copy invite link** — regrab the accept URL to resend it.
- **Cancel** — revoke a pending invitation. The link stops working immediately.

Invitations expire automatically after their expiry window. Expired rows show *Expired* — cancel them to clear the list, then send a fresh invitation.

## Members list

The **Members** card in Settings lists everyone who has joined the workspace, showing each member's name and email alongside their role. If a user record is missing a name or email, Maestro falls back to the next best identifier so the row stays actionable for admins.
